# RESONANCE: CORE
*A strange new game shows up on every computer in town. It's funny, it's harmless, and it has a cute little guide named HELPR. Then something breaks the Core, and the game starts coming out.*

A story-driven text RP on Roblox, inspired by **Homestuck**, **Deltarune**, and **Earthbound**.

**Worldsong Online** just appeared on every PC in **Stillwater**. Nobody installed it. Nobody knows who made it. Everybody's playing it.

*Labels: Rules*

## 📜 RULES

### General Server Rules
*Labels: Rules*

1. Be respectful. No harassment, slurs, or targeting real people.
2. Keep OOC drama out of IC, and IC drama out of OOC.
3. Follow Discord's and Roblox's Terms of Service.
4. Staff decisions are final. Disagree respectfully in a ticket.
5. No spoiling story events for people who missed them.

### RP Rules
*Labels: Rules*

- **No metagaming:** your character only knows what they learned IC.
- **No godmodding:** you can't auto-hit, auto-dodge, or ignore damage.
- **No powergaming:** stay inside your approved abilities, stats, and items.
- Respect the setting. WSO is a game; Stillwater is a normal town (for now).

---

### 🎭 In RP

❌ **Metagaming:** *"Jake walks up to Mia. 'I know you're a Taker of Rot, I saw your Trello sheet.'"* (Jake never saw her powers IC.)
✅ *"Jake narrows his eyes as gray decay creeps off Mia's hands. 'What... was that?'"*

❌ **Godmodding:** *"Kai dodges every hit and slams Ren into the ground, ending the fight."*
✅ *"Kai ducks under the swing and throws a wild punch at Ren's ribs."* (Let Ren respond.)

### Combat Rules
*Labels: Combat,Rules*

- Combat is **turn-based**.
- Every combat action **must** end with an **ability tag**. No tag = the action doesn't count.
- One action per turn plus movement.
- Use only approved abilities and items.

---

### 🎭 In RP

A good combat post has **3 parts**: what you do, how it looks, and the tag.

> *Ren plants his feet, Static crackling up his arms, and hurls a bolt of white noise straight at the Fluffbit's face.*
> **[ Static Snap | Hum | 8 DMG | 3 MP ]**

Describe the attempt, not the result. The target (or the GM) decides how it lands.

### PVP Rules
*Labels: Combat,Rules*

- **Anyone can jump or kill anyone.** No consent needed.
- **Fights in the Overworld are life or death.**
- **Villains are allowed.** Make one and go crazy.
- Kills only count if the fight used **ability tags**. Staff can review the chat log if there's a dispute.
- Certain PVP logs can earn **XP or instant rank-ups**. Staff watch for abuse (like trading wins with friends).

---

### 🎭 In RP

Jumping someone is allowed, but make it an RP moment, not just a hit:

> *Vic steps out from behind the arcade machines, cracking his knuckles. "Heard you've been running your mouth about me." He swings first.*
> **[ Basic Attack | — | 4 DMG | 1 Stamina ]**

The other player gets their turn to react. Remember: **in Stillwater, it's life or death.**

### Combat Logging
*Labels: Combat,Rules*

**Combat logging** = leaving while you're actively in an in-character fight.

Staff decide what happens **case by case**, since real disconnects happen.

### PVE Rules
*Labels: Combat,Rules*

PVE (fighting enemies) only runs when **staff are on**, or when **approved by staff/helpers**.

---

### 🎭 In RP

**How to get PVE:** ask in **#looking-for-rp** or ping an on-duty staff/helper.
> *"Anyone free to run a Fluffbit hunt for me and 2 others? We're at the Meadow."*

The GM controls the enemies and posts their attacks with tags too, so you can check the numbers.

## 🌍 THE WORLD

### Stillwater
*Labels: Lore*

The **Overworld**. An ordinary small town: suburbs, a main street, a mall, a school, a diner, an arcade. Nothing important has ever happened here.

You play your **real character** in Stillwater.

### Worldsong Online (WSO)
*Labels: Lore*

An **MMORPG** that showed up on every computer in Stillwater overnight. No publisher. No website. No update notes. Everyone calls it **WSO**.

You play your **Game Avatar** inside WSO.

### Logging In
*Labels: Lore*

1. Walk up to any computer in Stillwater and press the button.
2. The PC boots and the desktop opens.
3. Click **Worldsong Online**.
4. The first time, you'll build your **Game Avatar**.
5. You spawn on **Firstlight Isle**, high in the sky.

---

### 🎭 In RP

> *Mia drops into her desk chair, still in her school hoodie. The old family PC wheezes to life. A desktop she doesn't remember setting up. One icon: Worldsong Online. She clicks it.*

**While your Avatar is in WSO, your real character is sitting at the PC playing.** Their body is still in Stillwater, eyes on the screen.

Logging out works the same in reverse: you "wake up" at your desk.

### Firstlight Isle
*Labels: Lore*

WSO's starter zone: a peaceful tutorial island floating in the sky. Grassy cliffs, a spawn plaza with a fountain, training dummies, a starter shop, and the **Combiner**.

At the center, a ring of standing stones surrounds a big shining crystal: **the Core**.

Around the edges are huge glowing archways leading down through the clouds: the **portals** to WSO's other worlds. They're closed for now.

---

### 🎭 In RP

Firstlight Isle should feel **bright, safe, and a little too perfect**: soft music, warm light, cute monsters, HELPR everywhere. RP it like the first day of a new game you're hyped about.

### HELPR
*Labels: Lore*

WSO's tutorial guide. A small floating mascot with a round body, a static-filled screen for a face with one big pixel eye, tiny paws, and a "!" that pops over its head.

Hyper, cheerful, full of bad puns, and calls everyone **PLAYER**. Types in ALL CAPS.

> HELLO PLAYER!! WELCOME TO WORLDSONG ONLINE!!! I'M HELPR, YOUR HELPFUL HELPER WHO HELPS :D

---

### 🎭 In RP

HELPR is played by staff. It's **loud, helpful, and always in ALL CAPS**.

Players can talk to it about anything: tutorials, quests, where to find stuff. It'll answer with a pun.
> *"HELPR, where do I buy potions?"*
> **HELPR:** *"THE HELPFUL SHOP!! NEXT TO THE FOUNTAIN!! TELL THEM HELPR SENT YOU (THEY WON'T CARE) :D"*

### The Core
*Labels: Lore*

A huge shining crystal at the center of Firstlight Isle, surrounded by standing stones.

> THAT BIG SHINY ROCK IS THE CORE!! PLEASE DO NOT LICK THE CORE :D — HELPR

---

### 🎭 In RP

It's the centerpiece of Firstlight Isle. It hums softly, and standing near it feels calm. Players can RP touching it, sitting near it, taking screenshots with it. It doesn't do anything... yet.

The lake the town is named after. It's so calm it looks like glass. Locals joke that **it's never made a sound**, not even when you throw a rock in. Teens dare each other to swim out to the middle at night.

## 📖 WORLDSONG LORE

### The Songlands
*Labels: Lore*

*The official lore of Worldsong Online, as told by the game.*

> In the beginning there was silence. Then came the First Song, and from the Song came the Songlands: a chain of worlds, each one a single Verse in the great Worldsong.

The Songlands are made of **Verses**: Greenhollow, Brightwater Bay, Coinspire Market, the Endless Archive, and more "coming soon." Firstlight Isle floats above them all, where the first light of the Song touched.

### Wanderers
*Labels: Lore*

In WSO's story, players are **Wanderers**: heroes called into the Songlands from beyond by the Song itself.

> The Song is strongest when Wanderers gather. Play together. Sing together.

That's the game's slogan. It's on the loading screen.

### Parts & Notes (Titles)
*Labels: Lore*

In WSO's lore, every Wanderer has a place in the Song:
- A **Part**: how they sing (your **Role**)
- A **Note**: what they sing (your **Element**)

Together that's your **Title**. The game says a Wanderer's Part and Note can never change, because *"a song can't un-sing itself."*

### Notes (Currency)
*Labels: Lore*

WSO's money. In the game's lore, Notes are **tiny crystallized bits of Song** that drop from monsters and grow in the cracks of the world. They chime when you pick them up.

### The Core (In-Game Lore)
*Labels: Lore*

> The Core is the heart of the Song. As long as it shines, the Songlands are safe.

— Firstlight Isle welcome plaque

### The Verses
*Labels: Lore*

What the game says about each world below the clouds:
- **Greenhollow:** gentle forests and meadows, home of the round, mossy Hollowfolk. Keeper Fennick gives every new Wanderer their first real quest.
- **Brightwater Bay:** a sunny harbor of fishers and lighthouse keepers. Home of **Brinemaw**, WSO's most famous raid boss.
- **Coinspire Market:** the golden trade capital, ruled by the jolly (and very expensive) **King Pricewell**.
- **The Endless Archive:** a library that goes up forever and holds the history of the Songlands. The librarians shush everyone.

*All Verses are currently closed for maintenance.*

### Malcadence, the Sour Note
*Labels: Lore*

WSO's "big villain." According to the game, Malcadence tried to ruin the First Song by playing it off-key, and was sealed away by the first Wanderers.

The launcher says a **Malcadence raid** is *"coming soon!!"* It's been coming soon for as long as anyone's played.

## 🔮 MAGIC & ABILITIES

### Magic
*Labels: Mechanics*

Your Game Avatar's power, granted by WSO and built around your **Title**.

Magic costs **MP (Magic Points)**.

⚠️ **Whatever you pick for your Magic is PERMANENT and will shape your other powers later in the story.**

---

### 🎭 In RP

Magic is **flashy and game-like**: particle effects, sound effects, glowing runes, little damage numbers popping up. It's an MMO, so RP it like one. *"A blue ring flashes under Kai's feet as she charges the spell."*

### Custom Abilities
*Labels: Mechanics*

You make your **own** abilities. Every ability picks:
- **One category** (Offense, Defense, Support, Utility)
- **One tier** (Hum, Ring, Roar, Quiet)

Staff approve every ability before you can use it.

---

### 🎭 In RP

**Example write-up:**
> **Name:** Static Snap
> **Category:** Offense · **Tier:** Hum · **Cost:** 3 MP · **Amount:** 8 DMG
> **Effect:** A bolt of white-noise electricity fired from the hand.
> **Side effect:** None.
> **Looks like:** sparks gather in the palm with a crackle, then snap out in a jagged line.

### Categories
*Labels: Mechanics*

- **Offense:** deals damage
- **Defense:** blocks, reduces, or redirects damage
- **Support:** heals or boosts allies
- **Utility:** movement, sensing, or out-of-combat use

### Tiers
*Labels: Mechanics,Combat*

| Tier | Cost | Damage / Block / Heal | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hum | 3 MP | 8 | Start |
| Ring | 6 MP | 15 | Lv 5 |
| Roar | 10 MP | 25 | Lv 10 |
| Quiet | 16 MP | 40 (once per fight) | Lv 15 |

Defense and healing use the same numbers as damage.

---

### 🎭 In RP

Bigger tiers do bigger things **in the world**, not just bigger numbers:

| Tier | Damage looks like | Healing looks like | Size |

## ⚔️ COMBAT

### How Combat Works
*Labels: Combat*

- **Turn-based.**
- **Your turn:** 1 action (ability, basic attack, item, or Recharge) plus movement.
- **Turn order:** players go in the order they joined the fight, then enemies act. Higher Speed goes first.

---

### 🎭 In RP

**Example round (2 players vs a Fluffbit):**
> **Kai:** *Kai rushes in and swings her bat.* **[ Basic Attack | — | 3 DMG | 1 Stamina ]**
> **Leo:** *Leo throws a crackling bolt.* **[ Static Snap | Hum | 8 DMG | 3 MP ]**
> **GM (Fluffbit, 9 HP left):** *The Fluffbit bounces off Kai's face.* **[ Bonk | Hum | 8 DMG ]**

Then it's Kai's turn again.

### Damage, Block & Heal
*Labels: Combat*

| | Basic | Hum | Ring | Roar | Quiet |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Amount | Strength | 8 | 15 | 25 | 40 |
| Cost | 1 Stamina | 3 MP | 6 MP | 10 MP | 16 MP |

Defense blocks and healing restores the same amount as same-tier damage.

### 🎭 In RP

### Power Scale
*Labels: Combat*

**What damage and healing mean in the world.** Use this so everyone RPs hits at the same scale.

| Amount | Damage looks like | Healing looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **1–4** (basic attacks) | A shove, a slap, a scrape | — |
| **8** (Hum) | A solid punch, a cut, a small burn | Closes cuts, fades bruises |
| **15** (Ring) | A cracked rib, a deep gash, knocked off your feet | Stops heavy bleeding, eases a sprain, starts knitting a crack |
| **25** (Roar) | Broken bones, thrown through a wall | Mends a broken bone, heals bad burns |
| **40** (Quiet) | Fight-ending. Most people don't get back up. | Almost anything short of death |

**HP is how much you can take before you go down,** not how many holes you have. At half HP you're hurt and slowing down. Under 10 you're barely standing.

**In Stillwater, HP from food is grit, not healing.** Wounds stay until they're treated.

# PART 2 — AFTER THE ARC 1 FINALE

## ⚡ RESONANCE

### Resonance
*Labels: Resonance*

When the Core broke, your Magic bled into your real self and awakened as **Resonance**. People who have it are **Resonants**.

- **Mirror:** your Resonance has the same abilities as your Magic, in real-world versions.
- Works in the **Overworld** until the worlds merge.
- Staff can approve **alterations** later to make it better or more unique.

---

### 🎭 In RP

*"Kai wakes up at her desk with her arm on fire, no, glowing. Teal lines are burning across her skin like circuitry. When she panics, the lights in her room flicker."* Your first Resonance should be **scary and confusing**, not cool.

### Focus (FP)
*Labels: Resonance*

Resonance costs **FP (Focus)**.
- Base **20 FP**. Each Magic point gives **+3 MP and +3 FP**.
- Same tier costs as MP: Hum 3, Ring 6, Roar 10, Quiet 16.

---

### 🎭 In RP

**What FP feels like:** a full bar is a hum in your bones. Low FP: Traces flicker, headache behind the eyes, nosebleeds. At 0, your Traces go dark and nothing comes out.

**Resonance in Stillwater is the same tier scale as WSO**, but in real life. A Ring-tier hit in town really does crack a rib.

### Breathe
*Labels: Resonance,Combat*

Skip your attack to get back **6 FP**. Uses 1 Stamina action.

---

### 🎭 In RP

**What it does:** slows your breathing and steadies your Traces (+6 FP). **What it can't do:** heal you, and you're open while you do it. **Tag:** [ Breathe | — | +6 FP | 1 Stamina ]

### Traces
*Labels: Resonance*

Resonance shows on your body as **Traces**: circuit-style lines with thick block segments and panel shapes around the joints, like the seams of a machine under your skin.

- Any limb(s). Any color you choose.
- Proof the Core is broken. Your power came from inside the game.

---

### 🎭 In RP

Describe where yours are and what color: *"Teal circuit lines down her left arm, a panel shape at the elbow."* People in town **will notice** them.

### Traces: Flare
*Labels: Resonance*

Traces flare when you use Resonance:
- **Hum:** faint glow
- **Ring:** bright glow
- **Roar:** lines pulse and light spills past them
- **Quiet:** Traces go fully **dark** for a beat, then flare all at once

---

### 🎭 In RP

Always describe the flare when you use Resonance. *"The lines on Mia's arm go dark, and then everything lights at once."* (Quiet)

### Traces: Lv 20
*Labels: Resonance*

Traces stay the same size until **Lv 20**. Then they grow outward from your starting limbs, connect across your chest and back, and a few lines reach your face.

---

### 🎭 In RP

At Lv 20, people should stare. The lines reaching your face are hard to hide.

## 💥 CRASHES

### Crashes
*Labels: Crashes*

Losing in WSO isn't just a game anymore.

Going down in WSO (**PVE or PVP**) is a **Crash**: your Avatar is force-logged out and you wake up at your PC in Stillwater.

The Overworld is still **life or death**.

---

### 🎭 In RP

*"The screen goes black. Jun jerks awake at his desk, gasping, hands shaking on the keyboard. His nose is bleeding."* A Crash should feel **wrong**, not like a normal respawn.

### 1st Crash: Glitched
*Labels: Crashes*

Dazed. **No Resonance for 1 hour** real time.

---

### 🎭 In RP

RP being dazed: headache, ringing ears, your Traces won't respond for an hour.

### 2nd Crash: Injured
*Labels: Crashes*

A real injury you RP. Must be **treated before the next weekly event** (medical RP, Mender abilities, healing items, or a First Aid Kit).

---

### 🎭 In RP

Pick an injury that fits how you went down:
- **Minor:** sprained wrist, cracked rib, deep cut, concussion
- **Treated by:** First Aid Kit, Mender ability, or medical RP

**Untreated by the next weekly event?** You sit that event out. Free RP is still allowed.

### 3rd Crash: Corrupted
*Labels: Crashes*

Pick a **memory** your character loses, plus an **injury** that needs treatment the same way.

---

### 🎭 In RP

Pick something real your character forgets: a friend's name, how to get home, their own birthday. RP the moment they realize it's gone.

### Crash Rules
*Labels: Crashes,Rules*

- Crash count **resets every arc**.
- **No charbanning.** The only way you sit out is if an injury isn't treated in time, and then only the next weekly event. Free RP is still allowed.
- **Permadeath in WSO** only if you agree.

## 👽 LUMARI

### The Lumari
*Labels: Lumari*

An alien race whose real world was devoured. What's left of it is preserved inside WSO. The Lumari there are **survivors, not NPCs**.

Singular and plural: *a Lumari, the Lumari.*

---

### 🎭 In RP

Lumari are **loud, snarky, and competitive**, with grief right under the surface. Think: someone who roasts you nonstop because they're scared of losing another friend.

### Lumari: Look
*Labels: Lumari*

- Horns, with a shape unique to each Lumari
- Bright, saturated skin in any color
- Sharp teeth and glowing eyes
- Limbs and fingers just slightly too long
- Faces that go eerily still when they're serious

### Lumari: Culture
*Labels: Lumari*

Loud, snarky, and competitive. They **rank everything**. Insults are how they show affection. Underneath the attitude is grief.

Their homeworld, **Ilunae**, was built around music. Most Lumari still hum without realizing it, then stop themselves.

---

### 🎭 In RP

**Ranking:** *"Your horns? 4 out of 10. Your attitude? Negative."*
**Humming:** they hum without noticing, then stop and look embarrassed.
**Insults = affection:** if a Lumari is polite to you, they don't like you yet.

### Lumari: Names
*Labels: Lumari*

Day to day, Lumari go by **handles**. Their **real names** are verses from their own song, each with a meaning. They only tell their real name to someone they trust with their life.

---

### 🎭 In RP

Never give your real name casually. If a Lumari tells you theirs, **that's a huge moment**. RP it like one.

### Lumari: Echoes
*Labels: Lumari*

Small objects saved from their dead world. Each one holds a tiny piece of Ilunae's song. Losing one is losing a piece of home forever. (RP flavor only. Lost on a Crash.)

---

### 🎭 In RP

Describe yours: a cracked music box, a ribbon, a glass bead. RP holding it up to your ear when you're upset.

### Lumari: Typing Quirks
*Labels: Lumari*

Every Lumari has a typing quirk. It's how their voice comes through text.

---

### 🎭 In RP

**Examples:** swapping every "s" for "$", ending every sentence with "~", typing in all lowercase with lots of ellipses, capitalizing Every Word Like A Title. Pick one and stick to it.

### Aurenne-That-Remains
*Labels: Lumari,Lore*

The Lumari's old capital, **Aurenne** ("the bright city"), preserved inside WSO at the exact second their world ended. Towers of colored glass, bridges, concert halls. Blinding colors and **no sound at all**.

It's not officially part of the game. Its edges glitch, and the skybox is wrong.

### Playing a Lumari
*Labels: Lumari,Rules*

- One Lumari OC per player. Around 20 server-wide.
- Played in WSO instead of your Avatar. One set of stats.
- Same Magic system: Title, custom abilities, MP.
- No Resonance until the worlds merge, then they gain Traces.
- Lose an echo on a Crash.
